# Promo link icons

These marks identify the viewer's link destinations in the Basketbroom outro.
They are separate from Basketbroom's own game identity. Their owners retain
their trademarks; this directory does not put third-party marks under a
Basketbroom code or art license.

`sources.json` records download URLs, archive entry names, hashes, and any
conversion. All four brand marks came from the owner's public assets. No mark
was generated or traced with image generation.

- Instagram: `instagram-glyph-white.svg` and `.png` are unchanged files from
  the logo pack currently linked by Meta's Brand Resource Center.
- X: `x-logo-white.svg` and `.png` are unchanged files from X's toolkit ZIP.
  The archive calls its SVG `logo.svg`; inspection confirms its path is white.
- GitHub: `github-invertocat-white.svg` and `.png` are unchanged current
  toolkit files. `github-invertocat-white-clearspace.svg` includes the
  vendor-provided spacing around the mark.
- CurseForge: the exact `#logo` symbol from the official documentation site's
  sprite is preserved in `curseforge-official-sprite.svg`. The standalone
  `curseforge-logo-white.svg` wraps that same path and viewBox, setting the
  symbol's `currentcolor` to white. Its `.png` is a local transparent raster
  conversion, not a vendor-supplied PNG. Its canvas is 900 x 910; the mark's
  original top and bottom padding is retained.
- Website: `website-globe-white.svg` is an original generic line icon. It is
  not a third-party brand mark. Its PNG is a local raster conversion.

Keep the marks proportional, high contrast, and secondary to Basketbroom.
Place them on a quiet dark panel, not directly over a busy illustration. Do
not add gradients, glows, shadows, outlines, or other effects to the marks.
Use ample clear space. For X's account pairing, use a username near the
mark's visible height and retain space around the complete lockup. The
GitHub clear-space SVG is available when the layout needs explicit padding.
Keep each icon next to its own destination; do not form a sponsorship row.
Use a general "Follow the development" call to action for the social array.

Brand resources consulted on 2026/09/13:

- [Instagram brand assets and guidelines](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/)
- [X brand toolkit](https://about.x.com/en/who-we-are/brand-toolkit)
- [X brand guide](https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-brand-guidelines.pdf)
- [GitHub logo guidance](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo)
- [Official CurseForge documentation](https://docs.curseforge.com/)

The Instagram page separately describes approval procedures for broadcast,
film, and other specified media. This local asset preparation does not submit
or obtain external approvals. No dedicated public CurseForge brand download
kit was found; its original first-party documentation asset is used here for
identification of the user's CurseForge profile.
