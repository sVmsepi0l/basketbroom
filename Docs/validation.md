## limits and repeatability

pie integration tests alter transient copies and stop or restore them. the native suite arranges physical fixtures, then observes actual gameplay requests and outcomes; it does not inject scores, ownership, capture progress, or clocks. its isolated cpus do not establish full-roster autonomous match quality. flight checks inject movement input; physical key timing and flight feel need human playtesting. continuous physical e-key chase capture, difficulty, match balance, and the audio mix also need further playtesting.

the arena and hud have received visual review. the native riders now use the locally installed stock quinn simple mesh with an original seated flight loop and Teal/Copper material instances. the read-only native art probe passed **16/16 riders**, confirming mesh/animation paths, looping configuration, nocollision, owner filtering, team materials, raised hand supports and absence of legacy primitive bodies. reviewed front/side/three-quarter previews and the [actual native flight image](Screenshots/native-flight.png) show the resulting pose and cockpit framing. see [skeletal rider art](skeletal-rider-art.md) for the local stock dependency and fixture limits. this remains a recognizable stock-character foundation; finger contact, action animations, distant lods and sustained frame time need further work.

the original hurley has been reviewed in the [native hurleyback render](Screenshots/native-hurleyback.png), with wood/leather/ivory materials below the equipment cards and no central body occlusion. its provisional 103 cm length and shallow 36 cm-wide head do not fit the current oversized Bludger. it adds no striking mechanics, and no equipment-dimension certification is claimed. the original chase wings also have actual [Snipe](Screenshots/native-snipe.png) and [Snitch](Screenshots/native-snitch.png) renders after a measured obj y-reflection correction. their native component scale and nocollision configuration were inspected, and cosmetic flapping advanced. the snitch review waited for normal-speed practice release before an ordinary host stoppage. see [chase equipment art](chase-equipment-art.md) for source geometry, capture setup and remaining surface/hinge work. these art checks do not establish an aaa-quality or sustained-performance milestone.
