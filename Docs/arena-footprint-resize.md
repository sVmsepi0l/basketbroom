# Historical September 16 arena amendment

The September 16 amendment doubled the then-current enclosure length and widened it by one third, preserving height. The enclosure became 310.163 × 96.597 metres, with a 47.608-metre eave and 71.413-metre pyramidion apex. Both goal assemblies moved outward while retaining their hoop sizes, heights and spacing, and the behind-goal space stayed fixed.

This amendment was implemented in the standalone UE5 maps and is recorded in `development-checkpoint-20260916.md`. Native Creator Kit maps remained at the earlier 45% volume enlargement until September 25.

The current dimensions are defined by the subsequent [September 25 amendment](arena-footprint-20260925.md), which supersedes this historical footprint in both engines. The immutable source baselines preserve both revisions for staging validation.
