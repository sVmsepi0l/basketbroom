# Arena footprint amendment — September 25, 2026

The user's request to double the current arena is implemented as **twice the September 16 floor area**, retaining its aspect ratio and height. This interpretation was stated while an optional dimension clarification was pending. Each horizontal dimension grows by √2; this is not a doubling of every dimension.

The enclosure grows from 310.163 × 96.597 metres to **438.637 × 136.608 metres**. Its eave stays at 47.608 metres and the closed pyramidion net apex stays at 71.413 metres. Enclosed volume doubles relative to September 16.

Both goal assemblies move outward together. Hoop diameters, heights and spacing, the 5.093-metre space behind each goal plane, player and ball sizes, free-shot distances, and restart protection distances remain fixed. The generated C++ dimensions and the portable rule configuration share this target. Flight speed has not been increased automatically; traversal time needs playtesting at the new size.

The standalone stager starts from the verified September 16 source baseline, preserving its 905 actor identities. The Creator Kit starts from its separately verified earlier enlargement. Both target the same dimensions without sharing engine-specific packages. Every staging attempt keeps byte backups and checks the saved/reloaded collision geometry.

Red-rock landforms, the cliff alcove and coastal redwood scenery are repositioned around the enlarged court. Tree and adobe room sizes remain physically consistent. Environment cameras move back to retain an overview; decorative geometry remains non-colliding and outside the playable envelope.

See `development-checkpoint-20260925.md` for actual staging, gameplay and package acceptance. Source generation alone does not establish a working packaged game or complete Hogwarts Legacy integration.
